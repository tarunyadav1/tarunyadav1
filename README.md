## My journey

I’ve been building things since school. I studied Electronics and Communication Engineering for my Bachelor of Technology and worked with a team on an autonomous drone project in college.

I started freelancing in my final year, building frontends for blockchain teams. Through Superteam India, I found opportunities and a community of builders. That led to frontend contract work at DIVA Protocol and later frontend engineering for ZetaChain’s Layer 1 blockchain.

Alongside client work, I kept building my own products. In January 2025, I partnered with Pauline, who had a background in DeFi, to build FeedBuzz. We built and launched it over several months. People used it, but revenue didn’t follow. That experience taught me to think beyond shipping and pay attention to what people value enough to pay for.

I kept building, moving into AI apps including LoopMaker and AudioPage. Today, I’m focused on growing my own products and helping teams turn AI capabilities into software people can use.
